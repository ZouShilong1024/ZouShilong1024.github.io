Beijing Innovation Center of Humanoid Robotics

I received my master's degree from the National University of Defense Technology (2026) and my bachelor's degree from Dalian Maritime University (2023). During my master's studies, I was advised by Prof. Kai Xu and Prof. Chenyang Zhu. I currently work as a world model researcher at the Beijing Innovation Center of Humanoid Robotics.

My research focuses on Embodied AI (including WFM, VLA, and RL), Computer Vision, and Deep Learning.
